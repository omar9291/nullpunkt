Worlds of If · @worldsofif

One impossible question.
Every day.

  1. Episode 1

    What if the Sun disappeared?

  2. Gone in an instant.

    No warning. No sound. Just a ring of light where a star used to be.

  3. Its last light is still on the way.

    Racing towards Earth from a Sun that no longer exists.

  4. Nobody notices.

    Sunlight needs 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach us. Until then, it is a normal evening.

    Darkness.
